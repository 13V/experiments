How it works

Four steps. The pack itself opens about 20 seconds after you buy.

01

Pay 20 USDG

Approve USDG once. After that, buying a pack is a single click.

02

The chain rolls

Your seed, the operator's seed committed before you bought, and the next block hash decide the pack, about 20 seconds later.

03

Five tokens land

The pack opens and five Robinhood stock tokens arrive straight in your wallet.

04

Yours to use

Hold them, trade them for USDG on hood.fun, Pons, or pools.trade, or leave them sitting in Robinhood Wallet.

Pull rates

Every card in the set, by rarity. Five pulls per pack; each pull rolls a rarity, then a stock from that rarity.

Official rules, or why nobody can rig this

  1. 1. Your pack's result is keccak(operator seed, your seed, pack number, next block hash). You choose your seed when you buy. The operator's seed for your pack was committed before you bought. The block hash does not exist yet. Nobody holds more than one of the three.
  2. 2. Operator seeds are a hash chain with its root published at deployment. Every seed must hash to the one before it, and packs settle strictly in order. There is no second seed to try.
  3. 3. If your pack is not opened within 40 minutes, anyone can refund you. When the seed finally surfaces, you are still paid every prize the pack rolled. Withholding a win costs the operator the refund and the win.
  4. 4. Nothing you can change after buying is an input. Trade or gift a sealed pack; its result was fixed one block after purchase.
  5. 5. Pulls pay in the stock. If the treasury is out of that stock, or its price feed is off, the same dollar amount is paid in USDG instead. A pack never fails to open.

    

  


  

    
Questions

    

      

        Are these real stocks?
        
These are Robinhood's tokenized stocks, issued on Robinhood Chain. What lands in your wallet is the token itself, not a share in a brokerage account.

      

      

        What do I need?
        
A wallet on Robinhood Chain, 20 USDG to spend, and a little ETH for gas. That's the whole list.

      

      

        Can I lose money?
        
Yes: a $20 pack pays $17.18 back on average. It's a pack, not an investment.

      

      

        What if the operator disappears?
        
Anyone can refund you once 40 minutes pass. The pack still pays every prize it rolled the moment the seed surfaces, and the chain never lets anyone change a result.

      

      

        Is this legal where I live?
        
Packs containing securities are a regulated product almost everywhere. Knowing the rules where you buy and sell is the buyer's job and the seller's, not the chain's.
