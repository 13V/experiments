# What people on Robinhood Chain need, with the constraint that we launch on Pons V2

Read on 6 September 2026 from on-chain data, Robinhood's own endpoints, Bubblemaps' wallet
study, the trade press and the terminals' fee pages. The constraint shapes the answer: a Pons
token is a fixed-supply contract with nothing to hook, so a Pons launch can only own a need
through the contract that receives its fees and the product that contract funds and gates.

## The needs, ranked by evidence

**1. Stop losing.** Bubblemaps tracked 164,538 wallets in the chain's top memecoins: 102,929 are
under water, 63%. Profitable wallets realised $164.3M, losing wallets realised $162.4M, so two
months of frenzy netted $1.87M for everyone combined; 46 wallets made over $1M and five lost
over $10M. The scam wave is documented: honeypots, copycat tickers, "vanishing tokens".
Volumes are now slumping and last week's AMC meme fell 55% in a day. What the losing majority
lacks is anything that pays when a coin goes down, anything that limits a loss automatically,
and anything that tells them what a launch is before they buy it.

**2. Gas.** The base fee has sat at 0.32 to 0.47 gwei all week, twenty times the 0.02 floor,
so a swap costs about $0.25 and a busy transaction $0.70; spikes have printed $64 for one
transaction. Robinhood's wallet covers swaps above $0.50 until 29 September and never covered
Pons, GMGN, Uniswap, the dapp browser, transfers, bridges or third-party wallets. FOMO already
sponsors gas for its own users at 0.45% with a $0.95 minimum and takes 36% of terminal volume;
everyone else pays. From 29 September everyone pays.

**3. A yield layer, and the short side.** Builders surveying the chain in August found no yield
layer: USDG earns 7% inside Robinhood's app, stock tokens and memes earn nothing on chain.
There is no way to short any memecoin: Longbow takes them as collateral only, neither perp
venue lists them, and the only short on the chain is selling what you hold. Meanwhile the
trade press describes memecoin traders "trying to short squeeze Wall Street" through the stock
wrappers. The short side is where both the money and the drama are missing.

**4. Weekend execution.** Robinhood's published Saturday quotes had a median spread of 3.8%
across 194 stock tokens, with GME quoted 18.88 to 50.00, while the pools sat within 0.4% of
Friday's print. Anyone selling to the app on a weekend is being robbed by the quote.

**5. Clarity for stock-token holders.** Dividends arrive as a multiplier nobody's wallet
displays, the multiplier leaks (Ford's holders got 2%), the tokens are debt claims on a Jersey
issuer, and lost keys are unrecoverable. No tool on the chain shows a holder what they own.

**6. Launch quality.** Of 160,000 Pons launches, 1.1% reached a pool. Pools are thin, snipers
are priced in, and there is no forward view on whether a curve will graduate.

**7. Onboarding.** Largely solved: MoonPay sells USDG on the chain by card and Apple Pay, and
Across, Relay, LI.FI, Stargate and CCIP bridge in. Not a gap worth a launch.

## What a Pons V2 token can own

A Pons coin's contract can do three things with its fee stream: fund a product, gate a product,
or be the equity of a product that earns fees of its own. Passive treasuries fail the first need
on this list, because a treasury with a mascot does not stop anyone losing money.

| Need | Product that meets it | Fits a Pons launch | Our edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 3 | A venue to short and hedge memecoins, and to earn by lending them | Yes: the coin is the venue's equity, fees from interest and buybacks | Borrow the meme is designed and its oracle verified; Locate's contracts are built |
| 2 | Gas sponsorship for the users nobody covers after 29 September | Yes, as a paymaster funded by the fee stream and gated by stake | Timing; FOMO owns the gasless terminal already |
| 1, 6 | A safety and graduation-odds layer inside a trading tool | Only inside a terminal, which FOMO, GMGN and Axiom already are | The tape and the plumbing map |
| 4 | Routing weekend orders to the chain, and a board that shames the quote | As a feature, not a launch | Locate's premium board |
| 5 | A holder's view of shares, dividends and the leak | No: a tool, not a token | The dividend-leak script |

## The answer

The need a Pons launch can own outright is the third, and it is also the direct answer to the
first: the chain has 160,000 coins and no bear. A venue where anyone can short PONS, CASHCAT and
AI, where holders of those coins earn what the shorts pay, and where the coin launched on Pons is
the venue's equity, meets the two largest needs on this list with a product that is already
designed. Its second act is the gas need, which it can serve on its own terms: a paymaster that
pays the gas of anyone opening or closing a short, funded by the fee stream, so "short PONS, gas
on us" is live the day Robinhood's subsidy ends.

The runner-up is the gas need alone, a paymaster coin for the users FOMO does not cover, launched
on 29 September. It is real and it is timely, and it is also the one where the incumbent already
has the product. The rest are features to hang on the venue, in the order above.

The regime argues the same way. Memes are fading, volumes are slumping, and 63% of the chain's
traders are losing. Bear products are launched into exactly this.
